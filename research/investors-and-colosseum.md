**Judging criteria: two official texts, both VERIFIED, they differ.**

Official Rules s.8 (the contract; judged "by Colosseum and a panel of judges"):
1. Functionality: "How well does this Project Submission work? What is the quality of the code?"
2. Potential Impact: "How big is the total addressable market ... What will be the impact ... on the broader crypto ecosystem?"
3. Novelty: "How unique is this Project Submission's concept?"
4. UX: "How well does this Project Submission utilize blockchain to create great UX for downstream users?"
5. Open-source: "Is this Project Submission open-source? How well does the Project Submission compose with other primitives in the crypto ecosystem?"
6. Business Plan: "Is there a viable business that can be built ...? How adept is the team ... to execute on the vision?"

colosseum.com/hackathon (evaluation list on the live hackathon page): Founder + Market Fit; Insight; Product + Execution; Potential Market Size; Founder Communication; Viability; Traction. VERIFIED https://www.colosseum.com/hackathon

VERIFIED https://www.colosseum.com/hackathon

### 3c. Verdict (my synthesis from the verified items above)

- "AI x prediction markets" as ONE named investor theme: PARTIAL, weakly evidenced. Its two halves are each strong in 2026: prediction markets as an asset class (mega-rounds, Paradigm "breakout year", Coinbase Ventures PM infra, Galaxy OTC desk) and AI compute as a tradable commodity (Ornn $33M, Silicon Data $30.5M, CME listing 2026-10-05, ICE pending).
